    📖 The Lesson

    Stress is real — and you were never meant to carry it alone. The calm person's job isn't just to stay calm; it's to help carry someone else's load. Bottling it up only makes the cooker blow. Rest, talk, and above all cast your cares on God — that's the only relief that takes pressure out of the room for good.

    "Carry each other's burdens, and in this way you will fulfill the law of Christ." — Galatians 6:2
